📌 Dashboard Summary

This dashboard analyses user reviews scraped from Google Play for AI note-taking apps. The App Performance chart reveals which apps users rate highest and lowest (minimum 5 reviews). The Rating Trend chart shows whether overall sentiment is improving or declining over time using a 7-day rolling average. The Review Volume bubble chart highlights which apps attract the most engagement and how low-rating share correlates with average score.
